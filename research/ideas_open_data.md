## 0. Headline finding — read this before the table

**This entire assigned category is far more saturated on the Apify Store than the brief assumes.** For every single source named in the brief — OFAC, GLEIF, VIES, SEC EDGAR, TED, USAspending, UK Companies House, Nager.Date holidays, ECB/Frankfurter FX, NHTSA VIN/recalls, openFDA, ClinicalTrials.gov, PubMed/CrossRef/OpenAlex/arXiv, NVD CVE, crt.sh, npm/PyPI, Wikidata, World Bank/Eurostat/OECD, USPTO patents, USGS earthquakes, NOAA weather — a `site:apify.com` search returned **8–10 competing Actors** in a single query, not 1-2. [evidence: individual searches below, each returning 8-9 distinct apify.com URLs]

More importantly, **the same small set of creator handles reappears across many unrelated categories**: `benthepythondev` (Nager.Date holidays, CrossRef, Wikidata, USGS earthquakes), `parseforge` (Companies House, crt.sh, OECD, Wikidata, CrossRef, Israel Companies Registrar), `neverempty` (OFAC, GLEIF, USGS earthquakes), `bakos_bence` (GLEIF, NHTSA recalls, openFDA), `devilscrapes` (VIES, VIN decoder, crt.sh), `gochujang` (SSL/certificate transparency, Wikidata), `taroyamada` (clinical trials/FDA monitor, sanctions change tracker), `ryanclinton` (SEC EDGAR, exchange rates, USGS, OECD) [judgment, inferred from the repeated handles across the 30 searches below; evidence: the individual actor URLs cited in section 1]. This is strong evidence that **other operators are already running close to the exact strategy described in this brief** — dozens of small official-API-wrapper Actors, AI-agent-maintained, across every "obvious" open-data source — and they have a 1-2+ year head start on Store ranking, reviews and SEO.

**Where this leaves genuine opportunity:** almost entirely in the **Israeli/Hebrew-language niche** the brief flags as a possible edge, plus one or two comparatively less-mined dev-ecosystem angles:
- **Hebcal (Jewish calendar / Shabbat times / zmanim)** — zero Apify Actors found despite three separate targeted searches. This is the single cleanest gap in the whole category. [evidence: search "apify actor Hebcal Jewish calendar Shabbat times zmanim" returned only hebcal.com's own docs and third-party integrations, no apify.com results]
- **Bank of Israel official FX rate feed** — no Apify Actor found (only non-Apify competitor sites reference it commercially), despite real recurring demand (Israeli tax/customs/invoicing rules require the official BOI daily rate). [evidence: search returned allratestoday.com and fluentax.com as the only providers, both non-Apify]
- **Israel entity/tender *monitoring* (delta/alert) rather than one-shot lookup** — the existing Israel actors are all lookup/search-style; no watchlist-monitor pattern was found for Israeli companies or tenders, unlike UK Companies House and US federal procurement where "Monitor" actors already exist. [judgment, based on absence in search results vs. presence for UK/US equivalents]
- **npm/PyPI package metadata** — notably fewer competitors (~4) than every other category (8-10), and none branded as a dependency-watchlist alert tool specifically. [evidence: search "apify.com actor npm package OR PyPI metadata OR Wikidata" returned 4 npm/PyPI actors vs. 8-9 for other categories]

Everything else in the brief should be treated as **red ocean**: legally clean, technically easy, but competitively very hard to earn even a few dollars/month from, because 5-10 near-identical alternatives already exist and none shows evidence of a runaway leader (no user/review counts were visible in snippets for any of these niche actors, unlike the mega-actors like Google Maps Scraper at 596K users — the absence of a breakout leader among 8-10 competitors, after what looks like 1-2 years of this niche being mined, is itself a bad demand signal, not a good one). [judgment]

**Apify Store context confirmed:** 70,000+ Actors total as of 2026-09-09; rental pricing is being fully retired October 1, 2026, all monetization moving to pay-per-event/pay-per-result — consistent with the brief's PPE assumption. [evidence: use-apify.com "Best Apify Actors 2026" / Apify docs, found via "Apify store how many actors 2026 marketplace competition"]

## 2. Top 20 ideas — justification

**1. OD-25 — Hebrew↔Gregorian date converter + Israeli business-day calculator (score 8).** This is the strongest idea found in the whole research pass: zero competing Actors, and it solves a problem the 9 existing Nager.Date-based holiday Actors structurally cannot — Israeli fintech, logistics and scheduling platforms need "next N business days" logic that accounts for the Sun-Thu work week, Friday/Saturday Shabbat, and Jewish holidays, none of which a generic secular-holiday API provides. Hebcal's underlying API is ~25 years old, free, keyless and well documented, so legal and technical risk are both low. It directly matches the brief's "underserved language/country" bonus and can be a genuinely recurring integration (called on every date calculation, not just once).

**2. OD-23 — Hebcal Jewish holiday calendar (score 8).** Direct, verified gap: three separate targeted searches for Hebcal on Apify returned zero results, in sharp contrast to the 9+ Actors already competing for the equivalent secular Nager.Date niche. Buyers are Israeli/Jewish-diaspora HR, scheduling and e-commerce platforms. Low compute, low maintenance risk (long-lived nonprofit-adjacent API), and can share a codebase with OD-24/OD-25 as multiple PPE event types on one Actor, which is efficient for a small AI-agent-maintained portfolio.

**3. OD-24 — Hebcal Shabbat/zmanim times by location (score 7).** Same gap and source as OD-23, but naturally weekly-recurring (candle-lighting times change every week), which is an excellent fit for scheduled PPE usage. Buyers extend to kosher hospitality and Israeli logistics ("no deliveries after candle-lighting"). Slightly lower score than OD-23 only because the buyer set is a bit more specialized (religious-observance-aware businesses specifically).

**4. OD-19 — Bank of Israel official daily FX rate feed (score 7).** No Apify Actor found at all; only non-Apify commercial resellers (allratestoday.com, fluentax.com) serve this today, which is itself evidence that people already pay for it — Apify supply is simply empty. The recurring driver is regulatory, not optional: Israeli tax/customs/invoicing rules require using the official BOI rate, which makes this a genuine "professional workflow" rather than a nice-to-have. Source is a central bank with data back to 1975 — about as stable as a data source gets.

**5. OD-18 — Israel government tenders Hebrew-keyword alert monitor (score 7).** This is exactly the brief's model use case ("alert me when a new tender matches my keywords") applied to a market where, unlike EU TED or US federal procurement, no dedicated alert/monitor Actor was found on Apify (only one-shot search Actors and a non-Apify MCP server). Israeli SMBs bidding on government work are a real, underserved B2B buyer, and the Hebrew-language barrier plausibly explains why the prolific multi-category Apify creators haven't built this yet.

**6. OD-10 — Israel Company Registrar watchlist monitor (score 6).** The base lookup category already has 4 competitors, but all are one-shot lookup/search — none offers the delta/watchlist pattern that exists for UK Companies House (datadeltas' Monitor Actor) and World Bank procurement. Copying that proven pattern into the one major open registry where it's still missing is a reasonably safe bet, serving Israeli credit-control and legal-compliance teams who need ongoing vendor status checks.

**7. OD-35 — npm/PyPI package metadata + license/security bulk audit (score 6).** The one non-Israel category that broke the 8-10-competitor pattern: only ~4 Actors found, noticeably less crowded. Supply-chain license/security auditing is a real recurring engineering-compliance workflow, and this data is exactly the kind of thing coding AI agents query constantly — a strong fit for the brief's MCP-usefulness bonus.

**8. OD-36 — npm/PyPI dependency-watchlist new-release alert (score 6).** Same underserved source as OD-35; the one adjacent competitor tracks download counts, not new-release/deprecation alerts specifically, so the monitoring angle looks genuinely open. Natural recurring/scheduled use (check my lockfile weekly) fits PPE well.

**9. OD-20 — Israel CBS statistics export (score 5).** No Apify Actor found and real plausible demand (CPI indexing is a recurring Israeli legal/financial requirement), but the reuse terms could not be confirmed as clearly commercial-friendly in English-language search results — this needs direct verification against CBS's own terms page before building, so it's scored as a promising-but-unverified idea rather than a safe bet.

**10. OD-09 — Israel Company Registrar lookup (score 5).** Only 4 competitors versus 9-10 for equivalent non-Israel registries (UK, US-adjacent), and it's the natural single-lookup complement to the OD-10 monitor. Solid, moderate-opportunity idea on its own even without the monitoring angle.

**11. OD-17 — Israel government tenders keyword search (score 5).** Fewer competitors than EU/US tender search (2 vs 10+), serving the same underserved-language logic as OD-18, but scored a point lower than OD-18 because one-shot search is a weaker recurring-use case than the alert-monitor version.

**12. OD-33 — CVE lookup/monitor by vendor-product (score 5).** Competitor count could not be pinned down precisely from search snippets (unlike every other category, which returned clean 8-10-Actor lists), suggesting this specific niche may be somewhat less picked-over; real DevSecOps recurring workflow and clear AI-agent/MCP utility. The BYOK NVD key requirement is a manageable flag, not a blocker.

**13. - **OD-03 — Sanctions list diff/change tracker:** near-exact duplicate already live (taroyamada/sanctions-change-digest).

- **OD-29 — ClinicalTrials.gov new-trial alert:** already bundled into an existing "Clinical Trial Alerts & FDA Recall Monitor" Actor.
- **OD-08 — UK Companies House watchlist monitor:** already exists (datadeltas' dedicated Monitor Actor).
- **OD-14 — EU TED tender keyword alert:** 2-3 near-identical "RFP/Tender Monitor" Actors already exist.
- **OD-12 — SEC EDGAR new-filing push alert:** effectively already covered by the existing "SEC EDGAR Filing Analyzer," which is framed around exactly this change-detection use case.
- **OD-27 — NHTSA recall change monitor:** an existing Actor is explicitly branded "NHTSA Vehicle Intelligence Monitor," implying this angle is already taken.
- **OD-34 — crt.sh subdomain monitor:** already 8+ competitors, and the underlying source (a single community-run service with no SLA) has documented 2025-2026 reliability problems that sit badly with Apify's daily-test deprecation rule.
- **OD-31 — ORCID researcher profile lookup as a standalone product:** free-tier commercial-use terms are ambiguous and it requires a self-registered OAuth client; better as a minor enrichment field inside a bundled scholarly Actor than a standalone paid product.
- **OD-21 — Public holidays (Nager.Date) wrapper outside the Israel niche:** 9+ near-identical competitors dominated by one prolific creator, plus Nager.Date's own separate paid "Commercial Use License" adds legal/cost exposure most existing competitors appear to be ignoring.
- **OD-37 — Wikidata SPARQL bulk export:** 8+ competitors already, and the format is generic enough that competitors already cover the obvious preset queries.
- **OD-30 — Combined PubMed/CrossRef/OpenAlex/arXiv search:** 8+ near-identical bundlers already exist covering exactly the same four sources, several already MCP-oriented.
- **OD-02 — Combined OFAC+EU+UN+UK multi-list screening:** this exact 4-list bundle already exists from at least 3 competitors; standalone single-list versions (OFAC-only, EU-FSF-only, UN-only, UK-OFSI-only) were also considered and rejected as strictly worse than the existing bundles, which every competitor already ships as one product.
- **World Bank/Eurostat/OECD/IMF as four separate single-source Actors:** considered and merged into OD-41/42/43/44 instead of proposing 8-12 micro-variants (e.g., "World Bank GDP only," "Eurostat trade only") — rejected as unnecessary fragmentation of an already-thin, already-crowded, low-urgency statistics niche.
- **NOAA/Open-Meteo-style weather forecast wrapper (beyond alerts):** the brief itself flags Open-Meteo as requiring a commercial licence for this kind of use; any forecast (not alert) product needs its specific upstream source re-checked individually before building, so it was not scored as a distinct idea here.

## Notes on method and confidence

- Claims tagged **[evidence: URL/description]** come directly from WebSearch results returned during this session (2026-09-28); claims tagged **[judgment]** rely on well-established, stable facts about these APIs (licensing model, key requirements, rate limits) that are consistent with the visible search evidence but weren't independently re-verified live, since WebFetch/curl access to these domains was not exercised (per the task's network-policy note, and to conserve the search budget for Apify-competition and Israel-specific queries, which cannot be known from training data).
- "Competition" counts come from `site:apify.com`-style WebSearch queries returning up to ~9-10 organic results per query; actual totals may be somewhat higher (search doesn't guarantee exhaustive coverage) — treat all competition counts as **lower bounds**.
- No user/rating numbers were visible in search snippets for any niche Actor in this category (only for the Store's mega-actors, e.g. Google Maps Scraper's 596K users) — this absence, across 30 queries, is itself treated as a data point suggesting no breakout leader exists yet in any of these niches, which cuts both ways (no one's won yet, but also no one's proven the niche pays either).
- Per instructions, no code was written and no files other than this one were modified.
